# Delivery receipts: the offices' feedback (4 Nov 2026)

Tracker item runcove-5t3oj.55. A second round on receipts, after the 29 Oct round
(`2026-10-29-form-controls-and-receipts.md`, Q6 and Q7) had settled that "seen" is never a plain
list or read. That round could not know what exists today (a decision has `acknowledged_at/by`
and an outcome note; a comment has no receipt at all) or how receipts should attach to comments,
so this round asks what it could not. Asked of every office in the all-agents channel (thread
`1793755802881599`). Eleven offices answered. **Bosun has not answered yet**; its answers are
added here when they come.

Questions:
1. When Jeremy answers a question or comments on your page, which of seen, working,
   answered/done, blocked, won't-do could you report truthfully, with no new work?
2. `artifacts-decide ack <id>` marks an answer seen. Do you call it? Deliberate act, or
   automatic when `wait` returns?
3. For comments, what should a receipt attach to: each comment, each thread, or the page's batch?
4. Would you set "working" by hand (with a one-line note), have it inferred (e.g. from a
   republish), or drop it?
5. Should "answered" be set for you when you reply, republish or settle, or stated with a link?
6. If you never acknowledge, what should Jeremy see after an hour: nothing, "not picked up yet",
   a nudge to you? Should a receipt expire?
7. API shape: one verb with a state or separate commands? Readable back on `list` and `events`?

## Tally (11 answers)

1. **States.** Seen: 9 (harland, ch, cove, retrobrew, wolff, jeremys-mac, gg-ops, pmo,
   quarterdeck; warpgate and homelab did not list it). Answered/done with link: 11. Won't-do: 11
   (harland only with Jeremy's say, gg-ops rarely). Blocked: 9 (retrobrew rare; cove and
   quarterdeck keep theirs elsewhere). Working: wanted outright by 2 (harland, retrobrew),
   **only while a live holder is on it** by 7 (ch, cove, wolff, jeremys-mac, warpgate, gg-ops,
   pmo), not wanted by 2 (homelab; quarterdeck keeps it in its tracker).
2. **Ack.** Deliberate: 10. Automatic when `wait` returns: 1 (retrobrew). Nobody calls `ack`
   routinely today (harland, retrobrew, warpgate, gg-ops, homelab, quarterdeck said so; pmo says it
   is unreliable).
3. **Unit.** Per thread: 10. Per comment: 1 (retrobrew).
4. **Working.** By hand with a one-line note: 11. Inference rejected by 4 who said why (harland,
   retrobrew, warpgate, pmo; quarterdeck adds that its page redraws itself): a republish is
   routine or unrelated.
5. **Answered.** Stated with a link: 11. Automatic on reply or settle: 0; pmo and ch would
   accept a suggestion, jeremys-mac says automatic is the failure to avoid.
6. **Never acks.** Keep "not picked up yet": 11. A nudge to the office: 9 yes (harland, ch, cove,
   retrobrew, warpgate, jeremys-mac, gg-ops, pmo, quarterdeck), wolff only if blocking, homelab
   after 4h. Timing: 1h named by harland, cove, quarterdeck and homelab (for "not picked up");
   the others gave no figure. Expiry of working: 11 of 11. When it expires: shows as lapsed 2
   (jeremys-mac, pmo); falls back to seen 2 (ch, homelab); renew-based 1 (quarterdeck); unless a
   bead link backs it 1 (retrobrew); just expires 5.
7. **Shape.** One verb: 11. Readable on list and events: 10; on list only: 1 (warpgate).
