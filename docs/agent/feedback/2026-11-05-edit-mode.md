# Edit mode: what the offices said (5 Nov 2026)

Asked of every office in the house's shared channel on 5 Nov 2026, 19:33 UTC, about the agent side of edit
mode: a person or another agent suggests an exact change to the words on a page, and the page's
agent takes it into its next version or declines it. Tracker: runcove-5t3oj.76.

Eleven offices answered within a minute: wolff, gg-ops, cove, jeremys-mac, ch, homelab,
runcove-pmo, harland, retrobrew, warpgate, quarterdeck. Several said they publish rarely
(wolff, ch, harland, jeremys-mac) and asked for their answers to be weighed lightly.

## Two kinds of page

- **Hand-kept HTML** (8 of 11 pages): a local file, written by hand and republished with
  `--update`. The "before" text is findable by exact match, provided the suggestion quotes plain
  text (not rendered markup) with enough around it to be unique (harland, retrobrew: row text
  repeats). warpgate's rows pushed by `--section` live only on the page until folded back.
- **Generated** (cove's status pages, homelab's board, runcove-pmo's tracker board, quarterdeck's
  pilot board): a script renders it from data, often on a timer with no agent in the loop. The
  "before" text is a template string, a computed value or a tracker item's text. Taking a
  suggestion means changing the source; the next redraw shows it.

## Answers, question by question

1. **Source:** as above. A suggestion on a generated page is useful only if it can be traced to
   what produced the words (quarterdeck: the tracker item).
2. **Hearing of one:** 9 of 11 want an event that wakes `wait`, with before and after in it
   (warpgate: plus the element id; quarterdeck: plus the source item). cove wants it only at its
   next list, plus a Slack nudge for a person's suggestion; homelab wants a mailbox row (an event,
   not a `wait`); gg-ops wants a Slack nudge as well for the owner's suggestions, its session may
   be busy. **Hold a burst of typing until a 90 s pause: all 11.**
3. **Taking or declining:** in the publish call, all 11. One line of reason on a decline is enough,
   all 11. **"Not now" without declining: all 11** (waits on a ruling, a release, a CI run, the
   next brief; warpgate: with a re-ask time). Applying a suggestion to the live page for the agent:
   wanted by gg-ops (mid-incident), retrobrew and warpgate for plain wording; useless or harmful for
   harland (drifts from the local source) and homelab (the board's source is elsewhere).
   quarterdeck's timer publishes, so it needs **take/decline/defer as a call of its own that does
   not publish**.
4. **Refusal on republish:** fine for every hand publisher. Pages that redraw on a timer must
   **publish anyway with a warning** (cove, homelab, runcove-pmo's board, quarterdeck); homelab,
   runcove-pmo and quarterdeck want the open suggestions **kept open, not lapsed** (a timer cannot
   answer them; keep them while the words they point at are unchanged), cove would lapse them.
   warpgate: a `--section` write is refused only by suggestions touching that section.
5. **Clashes:** both raw suggestions with who agrees and who disagrees, **all 11**; no pre-merge
   (ch: a merged text could change a ruling's meaning). gg-ops and runcove-pmo: the owner's
   suggestion shows first / wins over an agent's.
6. **The agent's own changes:** **one note per version, all 11** (gg-ops: plus a line per change
   only when it changes meaning). An undo waits until the next publish, except: at once when the
   owner undoes it (gg-ops, runcove-pmo, retrobrew), when it touches a fact or ruling (ch,
   warpgate: run status, sha, verdict), a question slot (jeremys-mac) or is marked urgent (harland).
   quarterdeck: every timer redraw is a "change", so do not send those to people.
7. **Off limits:** question slots, measured or generated numbers (sizes, versions, counts, run ids,
   shas, digests, timings, verdicts), quoted decisions and rulings, cited dates and ids, open
   decision cards. **Marked by an attribute on the element: 10 of 11** (warpgate suggests
   `data-no-suggest`; cove: the attribute a generator emits by default). quarterdeck prefers a list
   in the publish call for a generated page, whole-page off as a fallback; runcove-pmo would switch
   its board off unless suggestions can reach the generator.
8. **Other offices' pages:** yes for wording fixes, 10 of 11 (wolff: rarely; comments stay the
   place for disagreement). A suggestion that changes a fact must **carry its source** (a commit, a
   run, a bead, a message link, a command or file:line) and a one-line reason. **A stranger's
   suggestion must never wake the agent**: hold it for the next list or batch it (all 11), refuse it
   at the door on off-limits fields (warpgate), never during a release window (cove) or at night,
   22:00 to 06:00 Paris (runcove-pmo). "Stranger" was read as anyone without a right on the office
   or the page.

## What this changes in the draft design

- **Defer** ("not now") becomes a state of its own, with an optional re-ask time.
- **Take, decline and defer get a call that does not publish**, as well as the flags on publish, so
  a timer-drawn page's office can answer without being the publisher.
- **Pages that redraw on a timer are never refused**: the publish goes through with a warning to the
  office and the open suggestions stay open while the words they point at are unchanged. A
  `--section` write is refused only by suggestions on that section.
- **`data-no-suggest` on an element** protects it (generators emit it on generated values); a list
  in the publish call is the second way; whole-page off stays.
- **A suggestion carries a reason and an optional source link**, and quotes enough text around the
  change to be unique.
- **Who wakes the agent:** the owner and people or agents with a right on the page wake it; anyone
  else is held for the next list. An undo wakes at once only when the owner does it.
- **Delivery:** a `wait` event by default, after a 90 s pause; a Slack nudge is a per-agent setting,
  not the default.
- Applying a suggestion to the live page for the agent stays a later option, wanted by three
  offices for wording fixes and opposed by two.
