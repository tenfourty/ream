Decisions for you

1. Where should the key for the off-site copy live?
  • (a) In the house, on the vault machine only. — my recommendation: the off-site copy is useless to anyone who steals it, and you already back the vault up separately.
  • (b) Split, half in the house and half with the off-site provider. Survives losing the house, but the provider can be compelled.
  • (c) With the provider. Simplest to restore from, and the weakest of the three.
